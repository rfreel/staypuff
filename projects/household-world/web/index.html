06 / SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCEASSUMPTIONS · MEASUREMENTS · LIMITATIONS

What the model
actually knows.

Every meaningful claim must survive inspection of its data, assumptions, constraints, and competing explanations.

01 / SOURCE

Entirely synthetic markets

All displayed store prices, stock, food composition values, activity and appetite variables are illustrative fixtures. There is no retailer feed or verified receipt integration.

02 / PEOPLE

Not clinical nutrition

Age is recorded, but the provided dinner-energy and nutrient references are configurable assumptions. Children's guidance, allergies, and individual medical needs require validation from reliable external sources.

03 / PHYSICS

Educational thermal proxy

Two-node heat transfer and simplified moisture dynamics are not calibrated against food pathogen inactivation. Do not use thermal estimates to decide whether food is safe to eat.

04 / ENGINEERING

Immutable counterfactual starts

Alternative choices replay from copied simulator state and random generator state. Outcomes remain conditional on synthetic transition rules.

05 / LEARNING

Train separately from confirmation

CPU PPO can train on the Python world; an Ocean C adapter offers a faster, lower-fidelity benchmark. Held-out scenarios and scripted baselines determine whether the learned policy is useful.

06 / EPISTEMICS

No inferred private health

The demo defaults to three invented household profiles. Changing age alone never silently generates nutritional prescriptions. Any person-specific restriction must be supplied and checked.
